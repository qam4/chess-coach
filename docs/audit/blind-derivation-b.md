[38;5;252m[1m## 2. Non-negotiable[0m[0m
[0m[0m
Three â€” P1, P3 and P5. They are exactly the properties whose failure produces negative rather than zero transfer.[0m[0m

P1, because a correct verdict with an invented cause is worse than silence: the student stores the cause. Everything else I would trade. A product could be vague, overloaded and repetitive and still be a net positive if these three hold.[0m[0m
