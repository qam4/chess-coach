The medium, exactly:

- The student is playing a real game. After each of the student's moves, the coach
  produces ONE short piece of written feedback about the move just played.
- No dialogue, no follow-up questions, no way to check whether the student
  understood. One shot per move.
- Roughly 80 words.
- The student is around 1200 Elo. They know the common opening principles and basic
  tactical motifs by name, but freeze when it comes to applying them to the position
  in front of them.
- A strong chess engine supplies ground truth about the position: best move,
  evaluation, tactics, threats, hanging pieces, candidate lines. The coach's job is
  the teaching, not the calculation.
- The coach cannot set homework, assign puzzles, or plan a curriculum. Its only
  output is this per-move feedback, during the game.
